A kitchen with one rule

Everything on the menu is made from scratch, every day — the pasta rolled by hand each morning, the ragu started before the doors open, the bread proofed overnight. We keep the list short so nothing sits.

Chef Marco Belline trained in Bologna and Florence before opening Sereno in 2018 with a simple aim: cook the way his grandmother did, for people who have time to sit and eat it properly.
